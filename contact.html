Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common support queries.

Where is my refund?
Refunds for cancelled one-time orders or paused subscription days are credited back to your HomeBite Wallet instantly. If you requested a transfer to your original payment method, it takes 3-5 business days.
What if my delivery is late?
If your tiffin is delayed by more than 30 minutes past your chosen time slot, please use the live tracking screen in your dashboard to call the rider. You can also chat with our support team for a delay-compensation credit.
How do I cancel my subscription?
There are no lock-in periods. Go to Orders > My Subscriptions and click "Cancel". You will not be billed for the upcoming month, and any remaining days in the current cycle will be delivered as normal.
