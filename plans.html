Subscription FAQs

Got questions? We've got answers.

How does the Pause feature work?
You can pause your subscription right from your dashboard up to 12 hours before your next scheduled delivery. The paused days are automatically added as credits to the end of your billing cycle.
Can I change my home chef mid-month?
Yes! We understand that tastes change. You can switch to a different chef from the 1st of the following week. Your remaining balance will be adjusted against the new chef's pricing.
What happens if I miss a delivery?
If you haven't paused your meal and our rider is unable to reach you after multiple attempts, the meal will be considered delivered. Please ensure you update your pause schedule to avoid food wastage.
Is the packaging microwave safe?
Yes. All our tiffins are packed in 100% food-grade, microwave-safe, and recyclable containers to ensure safety and convenience.

We get it. You want to taste the food before subscribing for a month. Order a one-time trial meal from any chef today!
